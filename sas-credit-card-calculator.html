Pro Tips

  • 1. Level points must be earned within a 12-month qualification period
  • 2. SAS Amex Elite is the most efficient card for earning Level Points (6 per 100 NOK)
  • 3. Use multi-card mode: Amex Elite where accepted + Mastercard for other merchants
  • 4. DNB SAGA gives up to 30k level points/year via spending thresholds (100k/200k/500k)
  • 5. SAS Amex Elite includes two 2-for-1 flight vouchers annually
DNB SAGA Level Point Thresholds

DNB SAGA and Private Banking customers earn level points based on yearly spending (calendar year):

Spend 100,000 NOK/year +10,000 Level Points
Spend 200,000 NOK/year +10,000 Level Points
Spend 500,000 NOK/year +10,000 Level Points
Maximum per year 30,000 Level Points

Level points are transferred when you reach each threshold. Transactions must be posted within the calendar year.

EuroBonus Tier Benefits

  • +25% bonus points on SAS flights
  • SkyTeam Elite status
  • Priority check-in
  • Extra baggage allowance
G Gold (45,000 pts)
  • +50% bonus points on SAS flights
  • SkyTeam Elite Plus status
  • Lounge access (you + 1 guest)
  • Fast Track security
  • Priority boarding
D Diamond (90,000 pts)
  • +75% bonus points on SAS flights
  • Points never expire
  • Lounge access (you + 2 guests)
  • Guaranteed economy seat 24h before departure
  • Priority rebooking during disruptions

Status is earned in a 12-month qualification period and maintained for 12 months after qualification, plus a 3-month grace period (15 months total).
