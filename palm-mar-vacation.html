About Palm-Mar

A peaceful coastal village in southern Tenerife, Palm-Mar offers a relaxed atmosphere away from the tourist crowds. Known for its authentic Canarian charm, excellent seafood restaurants, and beautiful natural beach.

Best Time to Visit

Tenerife enjoys eternal spring weather. Palm-Mar is pleasant year-round, with slightly fewer visitors from September to November and April to June.

Getting Around

The village is very walkable. Tenerife South Airport (TFS) is just 15 minutes away. Transport options include rental cars, taxis, taxi apps (PIDAxi, TAXI Tenerife), limited Uber availability, and local bus service (Line 111, 467).

Day Trip Ideas

Explore beyond the coast

Teide National Park

Spain's highest peak and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Take the cable car for stunning views or hike to the summit (permit required). The lunar landscapes are otherworldly. Don't miss sunset or stargazing!

1 hour drive

Los Gigantes Cliffs

Dramatic 600m cliffs on the west coast. Take a boat trip to see them from the water or visit the charming town. Great for whale watching too.

45 min drive

Masca Village & Gorge

Picturesque mountain village with stunning gorge hike (if open). The winding mountain road is an adventure itself. Get there early to avoid crowds.

1 hour drive

La Laguna

UNESCO World Heritage colonial town. Colorful streets, historic architecture, great restaurants, and a more authentic Canarian atmosphere than the south.

45 min drive

Anaga Rural Park

Ancient laurel forests, dramatic peaks, and isolated villages. Excellent hiking trails and incredible views. A completely different Tenerife experience.

1 hour drive

La Gomera Island

Take the ferry from Los Cristianos for a day trip to this unspoiled island. Visit Garajonay National Park (cloud forest) and try the local almogrote cheese spread.

50 min ferry

Getting Around

Taxi Apps

PIDAxi: Most popular taxi app in Tenerife. Download before arrival for easy bookings with upfront pricing.

TAXI Tenerife: Official taxi app with licensed drivers. Reliable for airport transfers.

Regular Taxis

White taxis with green light are available. Metered fares regulated by government. TFS Airport to Palm-Mar costs approximately €25-35. Taxis don't always accept cards, carry cash.

Public Buses (TITSA)

Line 111 connects TFS Airport to Los Cristianos via Las Galletas. Line 467 connects coastal towns. Buy Ten+ transport card for discounts.

Car Rental

Highly recommended for exploring the island. Book in advance for better rates. Major companies at TFS Airport. Drive on right side.

General Info

Currency & Payments

Euro (€). Cards widely accepted but carry some cash for small bars and markets. ATMs available in Palm-Mar.

Language

Spanish is the official language. English widely spoken in tourist areas. Learning a few Spanish phrases is appreciated!

Timezone

Western European Time (WET). Same as UK/Portugal, 1 hour behind mainland Spain.

Electricity

230V, European Type C/F plugs. UK visitors need an adapter.
